CouchCHD Receive

Send games from your computer to your Android handheld over home Wi-Fi. Leave the SD card where it is.

Download Android app Android 8 or later · Signed APK from the CouchCHD project

Connect before choosing games

  1. Install the download on your Android handheld. If it is already installed, choose Update. Update CouchCHD on the computer too.
  2. On your computer choose Dashboard → Connect a handheld. Leave that screen open. No library or firmware selection is needed to pair.
  3. Open CouchCHD Receive on the same home network. Choose the handheld on the computer, then Connect to … here. If it is not listed, try Find sender, or Enter host IP using the address shown on the computer.
  4. Compare the two codes. Approve on the computer, then choose Codes match here. A discovered device is not yet a connected device.
  5. Once connected, choose games from one platform on the computer. In Receive, choose the folder Daijishō already uses for that platform, then Receive files.

Keep both apps open until the receiver says the copy is complete. Then choose Sync for that platform in Daijishō.
