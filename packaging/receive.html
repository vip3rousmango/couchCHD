CouchCHD Receive

Send games from your computer to your Android handheld over home Wi-Fi. Leave the SD card where it is.

Download Android app Android 8 or later · Signed APK from the CouchCHD project

On your handheld

  1. Open the download and tap Install. If Android asks, allow this browser to install this app. You can turn that permission off again afterward.
  2. On your computer, open Dashboard → Sync to handheld, select games from one platform and choose Send over Wi-Fi.
  3. Open CouchCHD Receive and choose Find sender. Both screens must show the same code. Approve the match, choose your existing game folder for that platform, then tap Receive files.

Keep both apps open until the receiver says the copy is complete. Then choose Sync for that platform in Daijishō.
